Common questions

Getting started

How do I start playing?

When you open the app for the first time, choose Adopt a Tomomix to start the free built-in pet right away, or Import a ROM to play a classic toy you own. You can add more pets later from the gallery.

Does Tomomix come with classic toy games?

No. The only pet included is the tomomix, my own original pet. To play a classic toy, you import its software file (often called a ROM) from your own device. Tomomix recognizes which toy it is automatically.

Which classic toys are supported?

Color models: Plus Color, iD / iD L, P's, 4U and 4U+. Tama-Go and Tamagotchi Friends have experimental support: they run, but some features are incomplete and you may run into problems. First-generation black-and-white models: the original 1996–97 toys, Angel, Morino, Umino, Ocean and several other early models, including Digimon virtual pets.

How do I import a file?

Save the file to the Files app (on your device or iCloud Drive), then tap Import a ROM (or add a new pet from the gallery) and pick it. After choosing the file, confirm which toy it is if asked.

Everyday play

Does my pet keep living when the app is closed?

Yes. When you come back, Tomomix catches up on the time that passed. For classic toys you can choose how much catch-up happens (up to 1, 6 or 24 hours) in the pet's settings. You can also Pause any pet, and it won't get hungry, sick or older while paused.

I'm not getting notifications.

Open the iPhone Settings › Notifications › Tomomix and make sure notifications are allowed. Then check that notifications are on in the pet's settings inside the app. Focus modes can also hide them.

I can't hear any sound.

Check the volume slider in the app's settings and your device volume. If Respect Silent Mode is on, the app stays quiet while your phone is in Silent mode.

My pet's clock is wrong.

For classic toys, open the pet's settings and use Sync Clock to Now. Restart (soft reset), which is like taking the battery out, is also available there.

Your pets and saves

How do I back up or restore my pet?

Tomomix automatically saves a backup of each pet about once an hour while you play, plus daily backups for the past week. You can restore any of them from the pet's settings. You can also export a save file to keep somewhere safe and import it later.

Will I lose my pets if I delete the app?

Yes. Your pets are stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. Export your save files first if you want to keep them.

Premium

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no ads. It adds more than one classic pet at a time, Live Translation (beta), Ambient Background, fast-forward, photos and videos for classic pets, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and in-game points tools for supported models. It also helps support my ongoing work on the app. The original Tomomix game is always completely free.

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, then tap Restore Purchase on the Premium screen.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Live Translation

How does Live Translation work?

It reads the Japanese text on your pet's screen and shows an English translation in a bubble. The on-device mode is fully offline and private. For better results, you can add your own free key from an AI service such as Google Gemini. In that mode, a small picture of the pet's screen is sent to that service to be translated. Live Translation is in beta, so some text may be missed or translated imperfectly.
