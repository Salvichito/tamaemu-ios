# TamaEmu + Tomomix — brief for App Store copywriting

You are writing the App Store text for an iPhone and iPad app called **TamaEmu**. Everything below is a factual description of what the app does today. Please only promise what is described here.

---

## 1. The app in one paragraph

TamaEmu is a home for virtual pets on iPhone and iPad. It has two sides:

1. **Tomomix**, an original virtual pet game built into the app. It's free, needs nothing else, and you can start playing the moment you open the app.
2. **An emulator for classic virtual pet toys.** If you own one of these toys and have its firmware file (the toy's own software), you can import it and play that exact toy on your phone, with the original behavior, sounds and timing.

The two live side by side in one swipeable gallery, so a player can raise a Tomomix and their classic pets together.

The first screen asks the player to choose: **Start Tomomix** or **Import a ROM**. More pets can be added later.

---

## 2. Tomomix — the original pet

Tomomix is a classic care-for-your-pet game with 100% original characters, art, names and wording. It is fully free: no paid features, no ads.

- **Name:** one pet is a *tomomix*; several are *tomomixis*.
- **Look:** full-color, soft, cute chibi-style characters (not black-and-white pixel art), living in a cozy room with a window that shows the real sky for the time of day, and a meadow outdoors.

### Birth: catch a shooting star
- A new tomomix starts as a starry night sky with shooting stars passing by. The player **taps to catch one**.
- The caught star asks for the player's name, then falls onto the meadow and pulses, faster and faster, for 30 seconds.
- It bursts into light, and a baby appears. The player names it (up to 10 letters). Its name shows everywhere, including in notifications ("Mochi is hungry, Sam!").

### Growing up
- 19 characters in all: a baby, 2 children, 3 teens and **12 adults** (3 tiers × 4 types).
- **How you care for it decides who it becomes.** Good care leads to the "Excellent" adults, neglect to the "Bad" ones, and how well you discipline it picks the type within each tier.
- Age goes up as days pass (1 day = 3 years of tomomix life).

### Daily care
- Seven care icons: **Food, Light, Game, Medicine, Clean, Status, Discipline**, plus an Attention light that turns on when it needs you.
- Hunger and happiness meters (shown on a single status screen with stomach, heart and brain meters).
- Meals and snacks, weight, poop to clean (up to 8 piles, with little smell waves), sickness and medicine, bedtime and lights out (it sleeps with drifting Zzz's), misbehaving that needs discipline.
- A thought bubble shows what it wants. It wanders the room with little retro hops and jumps for joy when it's happy. Tap it when it's content and it hops happily.
- **Indoors and outdoors:** take it out to the meadow and back. It always goes inside to sleep.

### Mini-game: Follow Me
- A memory game. The tomomix looks left and right in a sequence, and you repeat it. Each round adds one more step, up to 5 rounds. Winning makes it happier.

### Controls
- Classic three buttons (A to move, B to confirm, C to go back), exactly like the old toys.
- Also modern taps: tap any icon or food directly, tap the screen to skip an animation. Players can use either or mix both.

### The ending
- When its life ends, the tomomix becomes a **fixed star glowing in the night sky**, with a faint image of it behind, its name and final age. Tap twice to send a new shooting star and start again.
- A **No Deaths** setting lets its life never end, for players who prefer a gentler game.

### Life on a phone
- **Time keeps passing when the app is closed**, like a real toy in your pocket. When you come back, it catches up instantly.
- **Notifications** tell you when it needs something, even with the app closed. The messages vary so they don't feel repetitive.
- A little randomness keeps it surprising (timers wobble a bit, misbehavior comes at unpredictable moments), but it's always fair: good care always pays off.
- **Pause** freezes it completely when you're busy: it doesn't get hungry, sick or older while paused.
- **Difficulty:** Easy, Classic or Hard.
- **Retro Screen** option: big pixels and scan lines, like an old screen.
- Its full-screen sky background is always on, free.
- **Screenshots and video recording** of your tomomix, free.

## 3. The emulator — classic toys on your phone

- TamaEmu runs the toy's original software, so the pet behaves exactly like the real toy, including its sounds, menus and timing.
- **The app does not include any toy software.** Players import a firmware file (ROM) they already have. The app recognizes which toy it is automatically.
- Supported toys today:
  - Color models: Tamagotchi Plus Color, iD / iD L, P's, 4U and 4U+.
  - First-generation (black-and-white) models: the original Tamagotchi (1996-97), Angel, Morino, Umino, Ocean and several other early models, including Digimon virtual pets.

### Features for classic toys
- **Time catch-up:** the pet keeps living while the app is closed and catches up when you reopen it, with a choice of how much catch-up (up to 1, 6 or 24 hours).
- **Pause** any pet.
- **Multiple pets** in one swipeable gallery, rearrangeable.
- **Connect Nearby:** two players with color models (Plus Color, iD, iD L, P's) can connect phones over the air to visit each other, like the real toys' link feature.
- **Downloads:** install extra in-game items (the toys' downloadable content, such as rooms, foods and accessories) on the color models.
- **Live Translation (beta):** many of these toys only exist in Japanese. TamaEmu can read the screen and show an English translation in a bubble, either fully offline and private on the device, or using Google's Gemini with the player's own free key for better results.
- **Fast-forward:** tap and hold to speed things up.
- **Ambient Background:** the screen glows with the pet's own colors, blurred behind the whole screen.
- **Screen options:** sharp or smooth pixels, an LCD effect with scan lines and a glass reflection like a real screen, light and dark mode.
- **First-generation models:** players can add their own background and icon artwork for a personal look.
- **Points hacks** on Plus Color, iD and 4U: add in-game points/money.
- **Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets:** see your pet's screen, and how long it's been since you last checked in.
- **Notifications** when a pet calls for attention, and optional reminders if you haven't opened the app in a while.
- **Automatic backups:** a backup of each pet is saved about once an hour while playing, plus daily ones for the past week, and you can restore any of them. Save files can also be exported and imported.
- **Sound options:** volume, haptics, respect the phone's Silent mode, and choose whether music keeps playing alongside.
- **Settings per pet:** game speed, stay awake, set the toy's clock, soft reset (like taking the battery out).

## 4. Free vs Premium

The app is **free to download**. **Premium** is a **one-time purchase: no subscription, no ads**.

| Free | Premium (one-time unlock) |
|---|---|
| Tomomix, with everything included | Everything in Free |
| One classic toy | More than one classic toy |
| Pause, catch-up, notifications, backups, settings | Ambient Background |
| Connect Nearby, Downloads | Live Translation |
| | Fast-Forward |
| | Photos & videos of classic toys |
| | Points hacks |
| | Widgets |

So a free player can have **one Tomomix plus one classic toy**.

The app's own message about Premium: it helps cover the costs of running and maintaining the app and supports its continued development; it's completely optional.

## 5. Audience and tone

- **Who it's for:** people who loved virtual pets as kids and want them back; collectors who own the real toys and want to play them on their phone; and newcomers who just want a cute, low-pressure pet to look after (Tomomix).
- **Tone:** warm, cozy, a little nostalgic, playful. Simple, friendly words. Avoid technical words like "emulator core", "firmware" or "ROM" in the main pitch; when the import needs explaining, say it plainly ("play the toy you already own").
- **Selling points to lead with:** a brand-new pet you can play right away for free; your childhood toy, exactly as it was, on your phone; it keeps living in your pocket even when the app is closed; fair one-time price, no subscription, no ads.

## 6. Please don't claim

- That the app comes with any classic toy's software or characters. Only Tomomix is built in.
- Support for Tama-Go or Tamagotchi Friends (not available yet).
- Features not listed here (online multiplayer, accounts, cloud sync, etc.).
