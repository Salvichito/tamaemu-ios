Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: TamaEmu doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your pets and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your pets, save files, backups, the files you import, your settings and any screenshots or videos you make are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. I don't have servers that receive them, and I can't see them.

What I don't do

  • I don't ask you to create an account.
  • I don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • I don't sell or share any information about you.

Optional features that use the network

Live Translation (online mode)

Live Translation works fully offline by default. If you choose to add your own key for an online AI service (such as Google Gemini), a small picture of your pet's screen is sent directly from your device to that service to be translated. Nothing else is sent, and it doesn't go through me. That service's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. You can switch back to offline mode or remove your key at any time.

Connect Nearby

When you use Connect Nearby, your device talks directly to the other player's nearby device to let your pets visit each other. Only game data is exchanged, and only while you're connected.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and I never see your payment details.

Notifications

Notifications about your pets are created on your device. You can turn them off anytime in your device's Settings.

Children

TamaEmu doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tamaemu.support@icloud.com.
